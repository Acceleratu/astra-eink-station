# Optical mask for the J101 USB-C connector

**Order two pre-assembled subassemblies.** The first includes a PET skirt of 125 µm, front adhesive with flaps, and two PET protective layers of 50 µm each. The second consists of a PET anchoring band of 50 µm with pre-laminated adhesive. Both are mounted on the PCB after reflow, outside the enclosure. At service, they are delivered together with the board. An industrial converter can produce prototypes from foil and double-sided adhesive tape, with cutting up to the liner. Material acceptance, tolerances, and quantities are confirmed by the converter's sample. [Micron Laser Technology Service](https://www.micronlaser.com/laser-applications/adhesives-and-tapes/) · [UV capabilities declared by supplier](https://www.micronlaser.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/PCB_Laser-Services-BROCHURE.pdf)

## Cutting and transferring the film

1. Place the materials, templates, and critical characteristics listed above on the order sheet. Request the skirt to be supplied in register on a transparent front transfer support, using two optical references or two device pins and a gripping tongue. Retain the release liner on the backside of the adhesive face until assembly. The two protection ears are already pre-positioned. Mark the support with the up orientation, indicating where the anchor is to be mounted.
2. The converter calibrates the UV laser on samples from exactly the same lot. Measure the effective kerf on separate straight test coupons in the X and Y directions, plus a curve with opening radius. The proposed process threshold is ≤0.040 mm, with variation ≤0.010 mm between measurements. Offset the trajectory in the waste by half the measured kerf. A carbonized curve, a detached wire, or a torn liner disqualifies the sample. Accept PET at ±0.050 normal on profile and PSA at ±0.015 on the critical edge, provided the first five laminates meet the following optical thresholds. Qualify these values on the material stack ordered.
3. On a common liner with common references, cut the two outlines of the tesa adhesive until reaching the liner. Remove the core and outer debris, retaining the integral ear portion attached to the support. Laminate directly onto the back of the skirt fixed on the transfer support. Reverse the assembly onto the support. Simultaneously transfer the two lateral PET side protections onto the back of the ears, then cover the central adhesive with the presentation liner. The central double cut creates a nominal 0.030 mm gap between PET and PSA. 0.120 mm segments are supported by the liner or the skirt throughout the entire transfer process.
4. Check the straight PSA strips at five positions on each side and continuously inspect the corners. The planar thickness of each segment must remain ≥0.090 mm, without any cut traversing the annular region. Superimpose the two cuts on an optical comparator. The adhesive setback from the inner edge of the PET must be ≥0.010 mm around the entire perimeter. The side shields insulate the two 1.23 mm tabs. A separation band of up to 0.150 mm remains inside the assembly, between steel and shield. It is located outside the connector trace and the user-accessible surface.
5. Provide a separate pre-laminated anchor with liner featuring a gripping tongue. Attach samples from the same lot for optical testing and for destructive testing on J101. Note the materials, lots, laser settings, kerf, and contour inspection.

## Bonding the mask to the connector

Clean the front face and the outside top of the connector shell with a lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol compatible with the PCB process. Allow the surfaces to dry completely. Keep the solvent, press, and adhesive outside the opening. Work at 20–30 °C in a dry environment. Use a J101 connector soldered to the PCB and a support that takes the load through its metallic enclosure. Tesa recommends a uniform pressure of approximately 20 N/cm² and indicates adhesive bonding development over a period of up to 72 hours for double-adhesive tapes. [Tesa assembly recommendations, page 22](https://www.tesa.com/en-gb/files/download/750252%2C3%2Ctesa-double-sided-bond-solut-2018-web-1.pdf)

1. Install a tubular USB-C gauge plug, without power, with mechanical compatibility to the J101 tongue. Secure the board and adjust the transparent support guide using a calibrated microscope and an XY stage. A standard plug has play in the receptacle. For the batch, a detachable sleeve can be processed to fit into the PET hole with 0.015–0.030 mm play per side measured and entering a maximum of 0.080 mm into a 0.125 mm film. The sleeve end must remain at least 0.095 mm in front of the steel. Only the standard tubular plug penetrates the receptacle. Align the fixture axis to the stamped contour of each part.
2. Capture the images before lamination. Acquire the inner and outer contours of the steel, the PET and PSA holes, and the outer surface of the PSA on the same reference system. Use transmitted and incident lighting, sampling ≤ 0.003 mm/pixel, reference scale and extended uncertainty pair ≤ 0.010 mm, target ≤ 0.005 mm. Keep the panel fixed and the transparent support locked in the measured position during liner removal and pressing.
3. Verify each pair of components. After uncertainty deduction, the PET opening must clear the entire bore by ≥0.025 mm, the adhesive must be set back from the PET edge by ≥0.010 mm, and the designed metal-PSA contact must have a continuous band of ≥0.035 mm. The radial eccentricity of PET–hole, including uncertainty, must remain ≤0.035 mm. Recentering is performed on the XY stage prior to pressing. Also verify the directional margin between PET and steel of at least 0.005 mm after uncertainty, with all transparent PSA, across the five perimeters, and the axial distance measured. Apply only if `release_pass = true`. On rejection, adjust the position on the XY stage and rescan. For a cut-out window that is too large, prepare a new skirt within the drawing tolerance. [Directional criterion and part correction](optical-receiving.en.md)
4. Remove the liner, position the skirt against the connector face and press with a force-limited compliant annular pad cut to clear the port opening. Nominal contact area on the metal is 2.096 mm². At 0.20 N/mm², the supported area takes about **0.42 N**. Hold for 5 s as the prototype setting, then support the skirt while gradually peeling off the transparent carrier. Lift the fixture and withdraw the plug along its axis.
5. With the skirt supported, form the band on a mandrel with an inner radius of 0.280 mm PET and apply it first to the back of the skirt, then to the metallic top surface. In the drawing, the fold axis is parallel to Y. The vertical surface of 6 × 1.425 mm experiences approximately **1.71 N**, while the upper surface of 6 × 2.1725 mm experiences **2.61 N** under the same pressure. Apply separate pressure to both areas and allow the circular joint to be free. The clamp transmits handling forces to the metallic top surface. Install the remaining connector shields according to the guide, then allow 72 hours at the mounting temperature prior to lot testing.
6. In the enclosure, measure the axial distance between the back of the rigid wall and the face of the skirt using optical instruments or rounded polymer feeler gauges. Accept tolerance of 0.05–0.40 mm and verify the vertical clearance of the PCB–display assembly. Nominal value is 0.1725 mm. On the lower portion, the back of the skirt extends to 0.0525 mm from the milled edge of FR4, while the nearest ear protection extends to 0.125 mm from the PCB. Before closing the enclosure, verify that the actual edge of the board has a minimum clearance of 0.020 mm from the straight skirt and that both ears are free. If the actual edge reduces this clearance, measure and attempt the alternative subassembly with a 50 µm apron. This allows use of a board with an advanced edge up to X −0.2075 mm, after verifying both clearances on the unit. At service, support the board and the glass and lift them together after release of the PV cabling and mounting brackets.

The variant for projecting FR4 edges has its own STEP file, templates and dimensional inspection. [Conversion with a front apron](repair/README.en.md)

## First-article inspection and testing

The comparator program reads actual contours in mm, ordered around the perimeter, and projects them onto the metallic flange plane. The parameters `case_step_sha256`, `pet_back_to_steel_upper_mm`, and `pocket_within_projection_bounds` link the case revision measurement to the actual axial position. The JSON file contains `serial`, `expanded_pairwise_uncertainty_mm`, and five lists of `[Y,Z]` pairs named `metal_bore`, `metal_outer`, `pet_hole`, `psa_hole`, and `psa_outer`. Record all images at the same XY position verified before pressing. The program intersects the two outer contours and connects the two holes to determine the common band along the entire perimeter. Four synthetic checks verify a nominal assembly and reject misalignment, a PSA profile entering clearance, and an excessively narrow metallic flange. |---|---|
| Material opacity | On an uncut sample from the batch, measure transmission from 420–700 nm using a spectrophotometer and a background reference. Accept at most 0.1% transmission at each measured wavelength. Shield the samples from side light. Opacity must be measured for this batch. |
| Electrical insulation of the material | On separate samples made from 50 µm film, 125 µm film, and Tesa laminates with PET, use a guarded megohmmeter with opposing electrodes of 1 cm². At 50 V dc applied to the sample, after 60 s, accept ≥100 MΩ, equivalent to a maximum of 0.5 µA. Record the humidity and laboratory temperature. Test the material sample separately from the PCB. [IEC method for volumetric resistance under dc](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62314) |
| Actual contact after bonding | Make five witness assemblies using the same conversion on real J101 connectors. After 72 h, check the adhesive footprint on a sacrificial sample by controlled peeling and microscopy, or by a transfer test on a replica without live contacts. Require an unbroken loop with no unbonded area crossing the full width of the contact strip. Use consumables that leave marks only on witness samples. |
| Retention | On a second witness sample after 72 h, pull the skirt along X with 0.50 N for 10 s using a wide grip outside the opening. Accept a permanent displacement <0.03 mm with no lifted corner or adhesive transfer. This tests the skirt. The metal shield and its PCB mounts carry the mating force. |

Recheck all five optical acceptance criteria after cycling. Compare the force with the same connector–plug pair before installation and with the GCT interval from the drawing for initial coupling. In areas where internal enclosure surfaces are being inspected, the background-corrected signal must remain below 1% of the open reference signal at the same exposure, without any saturated pixels. ## CAD cross-section and test limitations

The GCT model placed on the released board exhibits two flat, coplanar metal surfaces at X = −0.0025 mm. The outer metal contour is 8.94 × 3.16 R1.25, and the bore is 8.34 × 2.56 R0.95. The skirt lies between X = −0.1775 and −0.0525 mm. The adhesive contacts the metal surface and provides a continuous nominal band of minimum 0.08343 mm. The product drawing publishes the dimensional tolerances of the clearance. The exact outer profile of the mounting and the continuity of the adhesive on each physical part are verified by the acceptance method. J101 model by manufacturer · [Production drawing GCT](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/6501/USb4135%20Product%20Drawing.pdf)

The bore calculation conservatively offsets the entire modeled contour outward by +0.030 mm along its normal. Given an independent error of ±0.050 mm in the Y and Z center positions, and a cutting error of the PET layer inward by 0.050 mm, a minimum of 0.03586 mm remains on all normal directions. At a 5° tilt, the additional lateral displacement across an axial distance of 0.175 mm is 0.01531 mm. The remaining calculated value is 0.02055 mm. The support function for the rounded rectangle is `(W/2−R)|cos θ|+(H/2−R)|sin θ|+R`. For these symmetric profiles, the extremum occurs at 0° or at 45°. The method preserves the actual rounded corners of the opening. The adhesive contact area is accepted separately on the measured connector.

A straight ray coming from outside traverses two rigid sections with dimensions 24.30 × 13.30 mm and 9.70 × 3.90 mm, spaced nominally 9.65 mm apart. These sections limit slopes on each axis. Their projection onto the skirt plane simultaneously covers all angles from which the rear wall can be viewed. With tolerances of ±0.25 mm on the edges of the sections, ±0.35 mm between centers, the axial distance reduced to 9.35 mm, skirt-to-wall clearance of 0.40 mm, and a PET perimeter tolerance of 0.05 mm, the gap between the skirt shadow and the last ray remains a minimum of 0.711 mm in the Z direction and 1.25 mm in the Y direction. At the central opening, check coverage between the PET window and the steel at every permitted viewing angle, treating all adhesive as transparent. The bonded tesa loop and its black PET core provide an additional physical barrier. The connector housing has its own cutouts. These are separately covered in the final assembly.
