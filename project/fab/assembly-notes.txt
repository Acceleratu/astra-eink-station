ASTRA PROTOTYPE — FABRICATION AND FIRST-ARTICLE ASSEMBLY

Order a two-layer rigid FR-4 PCB. Finished thickness 1.00 mm nominal, 1 oz
outer copper, ENIG and green solder mask. The closed profile centreline gives
the finished dimensions. Keep plated and non-plated Excellon files separate.
Apply the IPC-D-356 list to the bare-board electrical test.

Use Standard PCBA, populated side TOP only, and the BOM and CPL supplied.
CPL coordinates and stencil DXF coordinates use the same TOP Cartesian
millimetre origin as the fabrication files. Add process rails on the ±Y
edges, at least 5 mm wide. Allow feed-in and out beyond the stencil steps.
Keep rails, clamps and adjacent boards clear of the +X radio overhang and
the USB and FPC openings. Confirm panel depanelization clearance before cut.

Use a framed, electropolished stepped stencil. Base foil is 100 µm. The DXF
layer FOIL_080UM specifies 80 µm near the RT6160. Layer FOIL_150UM specifies
150 µm around the Hirose FH12. Set paste apertures 1:1 to the TOP paste
Gerber. The DXF lines define foil thickness changes. Align the coordinates
to the panel map. Confirm the geometric step-to-aperture clearance before
cutting. Inspect paste deposits by SPI in both stepped regions.

Inspect the 15 WLCSP ball joints on U104 and the exposed-pad joints on U101
and U102 by X-ray. Reject shorts and missing joints. Check the FPC contact
fillets and anchor joints by AOI or microscope. The pin-1 and input/output
datums appear on the enclosed TOP assembly drawing. For L102 align the short
winding lead, beside the printed bar, with land 1 (RT_SW1). For U201 the
antenna end must point out over the +X edge. Keep the BME280 sensory port
clear of flux residue, adhesive and conformal coating.

Measure the tallest populated point from the PCB TOP seating plane. Maximum
allowed protrusion is 4.10 mm. Use the probe map for fixture clearance.
The battery, raw glass display and photovoltaic panel are installed after
reflow and electrical inspection. Their receiving requirements are listed in
off-board-items.csv and the prototype qualification procedure.

The removable 0-ohm links R124, R125 and R126 normally remain populated.
For current measurements, power the board down before removing a link and
connect the ammeter across the pair specified in measurement-links.csv.
Restore the correct zero-ohm part before the normal functional test.
